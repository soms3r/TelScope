The rules of the scope

Full text lives in the repository: TERMS.md. This page is the short version.

  • Authorized use only — your own numbers, written permission, or legitimate security research.
  • Prohibited: stalking, harassment, doxxing, fraud, unauthorized access, ToS circumvention.
  • Platform registration/recovery flows reveal account existence only — platforms' own ToS remain your responsibility.
  • Outputs are probabilistic leads, not proof.
  • Provided "AS IS" (MIT) — no warranty; you are liable for your use, including misuse against third parties.
  • Laws differ by jurisdiction (GDPR, telecom privacy laws…) — compliance is on you.
// privacy — digest

Nothing leaves your machine

Full text: PRIVACY.md.

  • No servers, no telemetry, no analytics, no cookies, no accounts, no phone-home.
  • All history stays in a local SQLite file inside your folder; Clear history wipes it.
  • Only network traffic: the wrapped tools querying public sources from your IP, plus one-time pinned setup downloads.
  • This site is static — the interactive background runs entirely in your browser.
