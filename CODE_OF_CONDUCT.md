# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (summary)

## Our pledge

We strive for a harassment-free, welcoming community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, empathetic, and constructive.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes; learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal/political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without consent — **including
  in issues, PRs, and test data**.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Any use of the project's spaces to plan or promote unlawful surveillance of
  third parties.

## Scope & enforcement

Applies to all project spaces (repository, discussions, issues, PRs). Report
conduct problems confidentially via the maintainer
(<https://github.com/soms3r>). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions and may ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
